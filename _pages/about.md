# Data Scientist

I am a Data Scientist with an M.S. in Data Science, Analytics, and Engineering from Arizona State University, specializing in Bayesian Machine Learning. My work combines statistical modeling, machine learning, data analytics, and visualization to turn complex data into meaningful insights.

I have experience working with healthcare, demographic, and real-world datasets, developing predictive models, conducting statistical analyses, and building data-driven solutions in Python and R.

## What I Do

- Machine Learning & Predictive Modeling
- Statistical & Bayesian Modeling
- Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Data Analysis & Visualization
- Healthcare & Population Health Analytics
- Business Intelligence & Reporting

## Featured Work

My projects include machine learning for stroke risk prediction, Bayesian hierarchical modeling of student performance, county-level population health estimation, and deepfake detection using convolutional neural networks.

I enjoy working on challenging data problems where rigorous analysis and clear communication can turn data into actionable insights.

## Technical Skills

**Languages:** Python, R, SQL

**Machine Learning:** Scikit-learn, XGBoost, Neural Networks, Model Evaluation, Feature Engineering

**Statistical Methods:** Regression, Bayesian Modeling, Time Series Analysis, Hierarchical Models, Statistical Inference

**Visualization & Analytics:** Power BI, Plotly, Data Visualization, Exploratory Data Analysis

**Tools:** Jupyter, RStudio, Git, Microsoft Office
